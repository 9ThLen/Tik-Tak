With a reference grid and oracle downbeats, the decoder often recognises the

several bars later. That is an inference from output traces, not a causal proof
of which internal state or threshold causes the delay.

## Consequence and next gate

Do not repair the M0c dominance rule retroactively and do not open S2. The next
experiment should be preregistered as a decoder-only change-acquisition
counterfactual on the same fully observable transitions. It should preserve A1
evidence and compare current state handling with the smallest explicit
state/reset or phase-reacquisition intervention. The endpoint is acquisition
latency in new-grouping bars, with current M0c as the frozen baseline.

No further corpus run should start until that counterfactual, its parity
contract and its treatment of the 62 censored transitions are fixed in writing.
