## Why this file exists

pointed at it. A reviewer working from the repository alone found the harness,
the preregistration and the tests, and no numbers — and `PREREGISTERED_M0b.md`
already opens by citing M0a's finding. A verdict whose evidence cannot be
reached from the tree is not reviewable, however sound the run was. This file
closes that gap: it records where the artifact is, what it hashes to, and what
an independent recomputation of it produced.

The verdict itself is not restated here. It is `band3_decoder_not_falsified`,
reached under the rule fixed in `PREREGISTERED_M0a_S0.md`, and it reproduces.

## Exclusions, and a hostile check the run did not report

All 23 exclusions are GTZAN; Harmonix lost nothing. All are `RuntimeError` and
all three distinct causes are decided before any arm output exists — no fixed
meter in the manifest, audio that is not WAV/FLAC/MP3, and reference beats
extending past the decoded duration. None is model-dependent.

The S0 review ran a worst-case check on its single exclusion. The equivalent
was not run here, on twenty-three, so it is run now: assigning every excluded
GTZAN recording the minimum possible paired delta (`A1−A4 = −1`) and `A1 = 0`
gives

| quantity | worst case | registered bar |
|---|---:|---|
| GTZAN A1−A4 | +0.5349 [0.5053, 0.5647] | lower bound ≥ 0.20 |
| GTZAN A1 | 0.9719 | ≥ 0.90 |

The exclusions cannot change the band.

Under the registered intention-to-treat rule, 19 GTZAN recordings produce no
phase in A3 and A4 and score 0.0; 3 do so in A1 and A2. The complete-case
intersection is 955 of 977 on GTZAN and 581 of 581 on Harmonix.

## The format-sensitivity guard did not test anything

`A1_max_format_sensitivity` is **exactly 0.0** on both corpora — not small,
zero, across 1,558 recordings and both perturbations. That is not evidence that
the oracle input format is harmless. It is the arithmetic consequence of the
perturbations chosen.

The preregistration withdrew an earlier triangular-envelope arm with the reason
that `BarTracker` takes a maximum over ±70 ms, so a triangle and an impulse
"are almost the same input to this decoder". Both replacements sit inside the
same window: alternating ±20 ms is well within ±70 ms, and a uniform amplitude
scale cannot move the position of a maximum. A guard that the decoder's stated
mechanism guarantees will pass cannot distinguish a robust result from an inert
test.

This does not weaken the M0a result — the band is decided by A1−A4 and by A1
against its own rotation null, both of which are large and were recomputed
here. It means one specific question is **open rather than answered**: whether
a hard 1.0 impulse at exactly the reference frame flatters A1 relative to any
evidence channel a model could produce. A perturbation outside ±70 ms would
answer it. M0b inherits the same construction and should not inherit the same
guard.

## Where the constraint sits — and a falsified prediction

Prediction P3 expected `A3 − A4` to be larger on Harmonix than on GTZAN. It is
the other way round, and by a wide margin: **+0.222 on GTZAN against +0.006 on
Harmonix.**

Read through the diagnostic map in the preregistration, the two corpora
disagree about which input is the limit:

| corpus | grid gap (A1−A3) | downbeat-channel gap (A1−A2) | joint (A1−A4) | sum of the two |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GTZAN | 0.350 | 0.400 | 0.571 | 0.750 |
| Harmonix | 0.430 | 0.257 | 0.437 | 0.687 |

**These are diagnostic gaps, not component costs.** The last two columns are the
reason to insist on the distinction: on both corpora the two single-substitution
gaps sum to more than the gap from substituting both at once. A decoder that
apportioned damage additively could not do that. Degrading one input already
costs part of what degrading the other would have cost, so no reading of the
form "the grid is responsible for 0.430 of the loss" is available from this
table. What is available is the ordering, and it differs by corpus.

On 30 s excerpts the downbeat-channel gap is the larger of the two and the
phase choice still has room — giving A3 the best rotation of its own grid buys
0.222 over A4. On full-length songs the ordering reverses and the best rotation
buys 0.006: `BarTracker`'s phase selection is already within six thousandths of
optimal for the grid it is handed. This is the registered "A2 high, A3 low →
the tactus grid is the constraint" branch, and it appears only on the corpus
made of the product's actual material.

## Population, and what it cannot support

Annotated meter in the primary set: GTZAN 920 of 977 in four, Harmonix 564 of
581. The result is a fixed-meter phase result on ~95% four-four development
material, which is what M0b exists to move past.

`BarTracker` emits grouping only, so denominator, `meter_family` and
`canonical_time_signature` are not measured. Room robustness is not measured.
The bootstrap resamples recordings, not compositions.
