## Diagnostic warning: zero cost is not the candidate

The cost ladder is non-monotonic. Removing phase-switch cost entirely makes the
control acquire quickly, but it is worse than `L2_latest` on the two-bar
endpoint, lowers stable exact-position accuracy by 7.25 points and increases
resolver/held-output changes from 212 to 3,829. This is the preregistered
diagnostic warning: a non-zero hysteresis budget is useful, while free phase
switching overreacts even under oracle evidence. The zero-cost arm remains a
power control and is not eligible for selection.

At the other end, `L64_latest` is exactly identical to the opening-phase
baseline. Thus simply changing the readout while retaining the existing switch
cost does not solve the delay. Cost 8 is also ineffective. The causal change in
this ladder appears only when the path-state hysteresis is reduced to 2.

## Consequence and next gate

Freeze `L2_latest` as the only M0d candidate and preserve M0d unchanged. The
next experiment should be preregistered before its first corpus output and
should run this decoder setting on non-oracle predicted evidence, with the
current decoder as a paired baseline. It must include static-segment safety,
transition reacquisition, false-switch/episode rates and baseline parity.

Until that regression passes, the result is not a reason to change the shipped
default and does not open any neural work. M0d identifies a decoder mechanism
under controlled evidence; the next gate must establish whether its gain
survives the errors of the real beat/downbeat frontend.
